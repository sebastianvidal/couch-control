Privacy Policy

Couchpad for iPhone, Mac and Windows

Couchpad turns your iPhone into a trackpad and keyboard for your own computer.

What it collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers. It does not collect, store or transmit personal data, and the developer receives no information from it.

How it uses your network

The iPhone app looks for the Couchpad desktop app on your local Wi-Fi using Bonjour, and sends your touches and keystrokes directly to that computer. This traffic never leaves your local network. The iPhone app asks for Local Network permission for this reason and uses it for nothing else.

Camera

The iPhone app asks for camera access only when you choose to scan the pairing QR code shown on your computer. The camera is used for nothing else and no images are stored.

What it stores on your devices

The desktop app keeps a random token for each iPhone you have paired, so you only enter the pairing code once; the iPhone keeps the matching token and the computer's address. Your settings (pointer speed and so on) are stored on the iPhone. You can remove tokens at any time with Forget Paired iPhones on the computer or Forget this computer on the iPhone, and uninstalling either app removes everything it stored.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
