Couch Control

Your iPhone as a trackpad and keyboard for your Mac or Windows PC.

Made for the couch: the computer is plugged into the TV, you're across the room, and you don't want a mouse and keyboard on your lap.

Free and open source. No accounts, no cloud: the phone finds the computer on your Wi-Fi, pairs once with a code or QR, and talks to it directly. Nothing leaves your network.

Download for Mac & Windows Source on GitHub

Set up in three steps

  1. Install the desktop app. On a Mac it lives in the menu bar as a cursor icon; on Windows, in the system tray.
  2. Allow it once. macOS asks for Accessibility access, which is what lets it move the pointer and type. Windows asks to allow it through the firewall on private networks.
  3. Pair. Open Couch Control on your iPhone on the same Wi-Fi. It finds the computer by itself; scan the QR code from the menu-bar or tray icon (or type the 6-digit code) the first time.

Hold keysWASD, space and F-keys that stay pressed while touched, for games

Games

Pointer movement is sent as raw mouse motion, which is what games read for camera look, and keys are sent as real key presses that stay down while you hold them. Some anti-cheat systems block any injected input; menus and strategy games are the sweet spot.
